A terrarium is a small garden grown inside a glass container. It's a beautiful way to display plants, needs very little space, and a well-made closed terrarium can go weeks without watering. The key is matching the plants to the type of terrarium.

## Open vs closed terrariums

![Closed terrarium](/uploads/2022/05/closed-terrarium.jpg)

**Closed terrariums** have a lid. Moisture evaporates, condenses on the glass and runs back down, creating a humid, self-watering mini-climate. They suit small tropical plants that love humidity.

![Open terrarium](/uploads/2022/05/open-terrarium.jpg)

**Open terrariums** have no lid or a wide opening. They stay dry and well-ventilated, which suits succulents, cacti and air plants. Never put succulents in a closed terrarium; they'll rot.

## Best plants for closed terrariums

### Nerve plant (Fittonia)

![Nerve plant with patterned leaves](/uploads/2022/05/nerve-plant.jpg)

Bright white, pink or red veins on small leaves. Fittonia loves humidity and stays compact, which makes it a terrarium favourite.

### Polka dot plant (Hypoestes)

![Polka dot plant](/uploads/2022/05/polka-dot-plant.jpg)

Speckled pink, red or white leaves. Pinch the tips regularly to keep it bushy and small.

### Baby's tears (Soleirolia soleirolii)

![Baby's tears ground cover](/uploads/2022/05/baby-tears.jpg)

A carpet of tiny round leaves that covers the soil like moss. It likes constant moisture, so a closed terrarium is ideal.

### Button fern (Pellaea rotundifolia)

![Button fern](/uploads/2022/05/button-fern.jpg)

A small fern with round, leathery leaflets. Unlike many ferns it stays compact.

### Golden clubmoss (Selaginella)

![Golden clubmoss](/uploads/2022/05/golden-clubmoss.jpg)

Soft, feathery, bright green foliage that thrives in high humidity.

### Peperomia

![Peperomia](/uploads/2022/05/peperomia.jpg)

Many small peperomia varieties suit terrariums. They handle humidity well and come in lots of leaf shapes and colours.

### Strawberry begonia (Saxifraga stolonifera)

![Strawberry begonia](/uploads/2022/05/strawberry-begonia.jpg)

Round, silver-veined leaves and little runners. It likes cool, humid conditions.

### Mosses

Cushion moss and sheet moss make a lush green floor and help hold moisture.

## Best plants for open terrariums

- **Succulents** such as echeveria, haworthia and small jade plants. See the jade plant below.
- **Small cacti** that stay compact.
- **Air plants (Tillandsia)**, which need no soil at all.

![Air plant](/uploads/2022/05/air-plant.jpg)

![Small jade plants](/uploads/2022/05/jade-plants.jpg)

## How to build a closed terrarium

1. **Drainage layer:** 2–3 cm of small pebbles or clay balls at the bottom.
2. **Barrier:** a thin layer of mesh or sphagnum moss to stop soil falling into the drainage layer.
3. **Activated charcoal (optional):** a thin layer helps keep things fresh.
4. **Soil:** a few centimetres of light potting mix.
5. **Plants:** start with the largest, then fill in with smaller ones and moss. Leave space between plants and the glass.
6. **Water lightly,** close the lid, and place in bright, indirect light, never direct sun, which overheats the glass.

## Care tips

- **Condensation:** a little on the glass in the morning is normal. If the glass stays fogged all day, open the lid for a few hours to let excess moisture out.
- **Watering:** closed terrariums may need only a spoonful of water every few weeks or months. Open terrariums with succulents need a light watering every two to three weeks.
- **Pruning:** trim plants that touch the lid or glass to keep them in proportion.
- **Mould:** remove any mouldy leaves immediately and air the terrarium out.

A terrarium is a great project for a desk or shelf, and a lovely introduction to the [humidity needs of indoor plants](/humidity-for-your-indoor-plants/).
