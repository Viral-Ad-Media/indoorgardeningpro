Indoor Gardening Pro is a home for practical, plain-English guides to growing plants indoors. Whether you have a single pothos on a bookshelf or a shelf full of herbs under grow lights, our aim is to help you grow healthier plants with less guesswork.

## What you'll find here

- **[Plant Guide](/plant-guide/):** which plants to grow, from easy houseplants and flowers to herbs, vegetables and terrarium plants.
- **[Plant Care](/plant-care/):** watering, light, humidity, soil, feeding, repotting and pest control.
- **[Greenhouse & Hydroponics](/green-house/):** indoor greenhouses, hydroponic systems and other ways to grow more at home.

## How we write

We focus on what works in a normal home, not a laboratory. Our guides explain the *why* behind each tip, so you can adapt it to your own plants and space. We update articles as we learn more.
