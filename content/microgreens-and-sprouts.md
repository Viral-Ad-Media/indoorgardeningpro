If you want to grow food indoors and see results quickly, start with microgreens and sprouts. They need little space, no garden and very little time, and they're packed with flavour.

## Microgreens vs sprouts

![Microgreens vs sprouts](/uploads/2022/04/microgreens-vs-sprouts.jpg)

**Sprouts** are germinated seeds, eaten whole (seed, root and shoot) after just two to seven days. They're grown in water, usually in a jar, without soil or light.

**Microgreens** are young seedlings grown in soil or a growing mat, in light, and harvested after one to three weeks when they have their first leaves. You cut them above the soil and eat only the stems and leaves.

| | Sprouts | Microgreens |
|---|---|---|
| Time | 2–7 days | 7–21 days |
| Grown in | Water (jar or sprouter) | Soil, coir or mat |
| Light needed | No | Yes |
| Part eaten | Whole sprout | Stems and leaves |

## Best seeds to start with

**For microgreens:** radish (fast and spicy), broccoli, pea shoots, sunflower, mustard, rocket (arugula), red cabbage, beetroot, coriander and basil.

**For sprouts:** alfalfa, mung bean, lentil, broccoli and chickpea.

Always buy seeds sold specifically for sprouting or microgreens. They are produced to higher hygiene standards and are not treated with chemicals.

## How to grow microgreens

![Microgreens in a tray](/uploads/2022/04/microgreens.jpg)

1. **Fill a shallow tray** with 2–3 cm of moist potting mix or a coir growing mat. The tray needs drainage holes, or use a mat in a tray without holes and water carefully.
2. **Scatter seeds thickly** and evenly across the surface. Press them in gently.
3. **Cover** with another tray or lid for two to four days to keep them dark and moist while they germinate. Some growers add a little weight on top for even growth.
4. **Uncover and give light** once they've sprouted: a bright window or a grow light for 12–16 hours a day.
5. **Water from below** by pouring water into an outer tray, so the seedlings stay dry and don't rot.
6. **Harvest** with clean scissors once the first leaves have opened, usually after 7–21 days.

## How to grow sprouts in a jar

![Sprouts in a jar](/uploads/2022/04/sprouts.jpg)

1. **Soak** one to two tablespoons of seeds in cool water for 6–12 hours.
2. **Drain** through a sprouting lid or mesh.
3. **Rinse and drain twice a day**, leaving the jar tilted upside down so water can drain away.
4. **Harvest** after two to seven days, depending on the seed. Give them a final rinse and refrigerate.

## Food safety

Sprouts are grown in warm, moist conditions, which can also suit harmful bacteria. To reduce the risk:

- Use seeds sold for sprouting.
- Use clean equipment and rinse sprouts at least twice a day with clean water.
- Discard any that smell bad or look slimy.
- Refrigerate after harvesting and eat within a few days.
- Health authorities advise that young children, older adults, pregnant women and people with weakened immune systems avoid raw sprouts and eat them only thoroughly cooked.

Microgreens, grown in light and cut above the soil, carry less of this risk, but the same hygiene rules apply.

## Troubleshooting

- **Mould at the base:** too wet, too warm or poor airflow. Water from below, use a fan, and sow slightly less thickly.
- **Leggy, pale microgreens:** not enough light.
- **Uneven germination:** seeds not kept evenly moist and dark at the start.
