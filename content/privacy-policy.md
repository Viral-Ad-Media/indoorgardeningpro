This policy explains what information Indoor Gardening Pro ("we", "us") collects when you visit indoorgardeningpro.com, and how we use it.

## Information we collect

- **Usage data.** Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of your visit. We use this to keep the site secure and working well.
- **Information you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.
- **Editor accounts.** If you are a site editor, we store your name, email address and a securely hashed password so you can sign in.

## Cookies

Visitors to the public site are not tracked with advertising cookies by us. Editors who sign in receive a session cookie that keeps them logged in. If we add analytics or advertising in the future, we will update this policy.

## Affiliate links

Some links on this site go to retailers such as Amazon. When you click them, those retailers may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies. See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## How we use information

We use information only to run and improve this website, reply to messages, and keep the site secure. We do not sell your personal information.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, [contact us](/contact/).

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
